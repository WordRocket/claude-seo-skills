Keyword research, a rank check, competitor research and a backlink check, run from a Claude chat on DataForSEO's pay-as-you-go API. What you need

  • A DataForSEO account. New accounts get a $1 free credit for testing. After that, top-ups start at $50 and the balance never expires. (Help Center, pricing)
  • Claude with custom connectors. DataForSEO's setup guide lists a Claude Pro plan as the prerequisite.

Setup (5 minutes)

  1. Create your account at dataforseo.com.
  2. In Claude: Customize › Connectors › Add custom connector. Name: DataForSEO. URL: https://mcp.dataforseo.com/v3/mcp. Add it, then approve the authorization screen (you sign in to DataForSEO; no API key goes into Claude). (DataForSEO's guide)
  3. Optional: add the skill: Customize › Skills › + Add › Upload skill and pick dataforseo-seo-tool.zip from the free repo's dist/ folder.
  4. Open a new chat, make sure the DataForSEO connector is on, and paste prompt 1.

No connector? Sign in at app.dataforseo.com, open API Playground, pick the endpoint, paste the request (the free skill writes it for you), press Send. Every response shows its cost. Paste the response into Claude and ask for the report. Never paste your API password into a chat.

The 4 prompts

Swap the [brackets] for your own keyword, site and country.

1. Keyword research

Use DataForSEO (Labs keyword suggestions, live) for the seed keyword "[ai blog writer]", location [United States], language English. Limit 20, sorted by search volume. Tell me the estimated cost before you call it. Show a table: keyword, monthly search volume, keyword difficulty. Mark the 5 best targets for [my site and what it sells]. Then tell me the actual cost from the response. Don't invent any numbers.

2. Rank tracking

Use DataForSEO (Google organic SERP, live advanced) for "[ai blog writer]", [United States], English, desktop, depth 100. Estimate the cost first. Tell me where [mysite.com] ranks (position and URL) or say it's not in the top 100, list the top 10 domains and the SERP features (AI Overview, People Also Ask, local pack). Then the actual cost.

3. Competitors

Use DataForSEO (Labs competitors domain, live) for [mysite.com], [United States], English, exclude top domains, limit 10, sorted by shared keywords. Estimate the cost first. Table: domain, shared keywords, and whether each one looks like a real competitor. Then the actual cost.

4. Backlinks

Use DataForSEO (Backlinks summary, live) for [mysite.com], including subdomains, live links only. Estimate the cost first. Give me backlinks, referring domains, rank, spam score and first seen, in plain words. Then the actual cost.

Then put it together

Put the four results into one report: keywords, where I rank, top competitors, backlink summary, and the 3 biggest gaps (each with the number behind it and the page or fix that closes it). Add a table of what each call cost. Only use numbers from the responses above.

The honest notes

  • The free dollar covers this test about 14 times ($0.067 each). After that, the smallest top-up is $50 (it never expires).
  • Not like-for-like with Semrush or Ahrefs. Semrush's SEO plan is $139/mo and Ahrefs Lite is $129/mo (list prices, billed monthly, checked Nov 13, 2026). They include dashboards, history, site audits and tracking projects. Here you get raw data on demand and Claude builds the view.
  • Costs grow with limits. 20 keyword ideas cost $0.0144; 1,000 cost about $0.13. Twenty rank checks at depth 100 cost about $0.31. Ask Claude for the estimate first (the free skill does it automatically and asks before anything over $0.25).
  • It's DataForSEO's data. Search volumes are monthly estimates; a ranking is one check at one place and time, and it changes day to day.
  • Never let a chat top up or change billing. Do that yourself in your DataForSEO account.
