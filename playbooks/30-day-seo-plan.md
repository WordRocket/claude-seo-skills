# The 30-day SEO plan

One task a day, 15 to 30 minutes, for a month. It starts with the pages Google already almost ranks, then AI answers,
then links, and ends with what to measure. Same plan is in the free SEO tracker (sheet "30 days") with a Done column.
Checked November 2026. Nobody can promise rankings: these are the habits that give a site its best shot.

## Week 1: quick wins and switches

| When | Task | Free help |
|---|---|---|

| Day 2 | Fix shortlist page 1: answer first, add what the top 5 don't have, link to it from 2–3 related pages. Request indexing once. | ranking-principles skill |

## What to measure (Google's own reports)

- **Performance report:** clicks, impressions, CTR and average position. Google: "Impressions and clicks are
  ultimately the measure of success for your site."
- **Page indexing report:** which pages Google indexed and why others weren't. Google says sites under 500 pages
  "probably don't need to use this report": spot-check key pages with URL Inspection instead.
- **Core Web Vitals report:** real-user speed and stability data (LCP, INP, CLS), mobile and desktop.
- **Manual actions and Security issues:** should both say no issues. Check them first if traffic falls off a cliff.

**Comparing with last year?** Google confirmed Search Console misreported impressions (and so CTR and average position)
from May 13, 2025 to April 27, 2026. Clicks weren't affected, so compare clicks for those dates.

**Be patient.** Google says indexing a new page can take "a week or so", and to wait "a few weeks" before judging your
work. After a core update, recovery "could take several months".
